Our Philosophy

AI that augments expertise.
Not replaces it.

We build at the intersection of generative, agentic and deterministic reasoning — and we are deliberate about where each belongs. Models accelerate synthesis and orchestrate the work; deterministic rules and decades of domain logic hold them to clinical and biological reality.

We don't believe AI should replace scientific expertise in drug development. The complexity of oncology and the stakes of each decision demand a human who understands the science and owns the process as much as the outcome. With a fully visible corpus and every output traceable to source, the expert stays in control throughout.

That means striking the right balance between control and delegation at every layer of the platform. We'd love to share how we think about it.
